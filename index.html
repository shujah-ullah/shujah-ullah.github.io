About

Alongside that, I completed a BS in Computer Software Engineering (Virtual University of Pakistan, 2021–2025) and an EduQual UK RQF Level 6 diploma in Artificial Intelligence Operations (AIOps) — the discipline that fuses DevOps, DevSecOps and cloud with machine learning.
